Under the hood

Trained on real student lifestyle data

The predictor is powered by a supervised regression model trained on a global survey of student social-media habits, study routines, sleep and self-reported stress — served through a lightweight FastAPI backend.

Dataset

Thousands of anonymized student records spanning 10+ countries, platforms and academic levels.

ML model

Gradient-boosted regression trained on lifestyle & usage features, served via a FastAPI endpoint.

Features used by the model

  • Age
  • Gender
  • Country
  • Academic level
  • Most used platform
  • Purpose of use
  • Avg. daily usage hours
  • Daily unlocks
  • Study hours
  • Physical activity hours
  • Sleep hours per night
  • Stress level
